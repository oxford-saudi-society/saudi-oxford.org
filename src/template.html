Our community

A Saudi community in Oxford

Oxford Saudi Society brings together undergraduates, graduates, researchers, alumni and friends. Membership is open to all members of the University — you do not need to be Saudi to join.

Founded in [year], the Society runs a termly programme of cultural, social and academic events, and works with colleges and other societies across the University.

What we hold to

  • Welcoming and inclusive — people first, clear invitations and useful information.
  • Collegiate and trustworthy — thoughtful, composed, well organised.
  • Proud of Saudi heritage — expressed with care and hospitality.
  • Curious and connected — we encourage conversations across cultures and disciplines.

FAQ

Before you ask

Do I have to be Saudi to join?

No. Membership is open to all members of the University, and our cultural events are open to the wider Oxford community. Membership is free for Saudi students; other members pay a small annual fee that funds the programme.

How do I prove I'm Saudi?

After you apply we'll email you for a photo of your Saudi passport or national ID, or your Cultural Bureau scholarship confirmation. We check it and then delete it — no copy is kept.

How do I pay the membership fee?

Once you've applied you get a reference number, and you can pay by card through a secure payment page or by bank transfer quoting the reference. Membership starts as soon as the payment is matched. A hardship fund exists for anyone who needs it — email the treasurer in confidence.

Can I come along before joining?

Yes. All open events are free to non-members. Join whenever you have decided.

Are alumni and staff welcome?

Alumni and staff are welcome at social events and the annual dinner. Full membership may be restricted by the Society's constitution.

How do I hear about events?

The programme is published at the start of each term, emailed to members and posted on our social channels.

Are the venues accessible?

Access details are listed with each event. If you need an adjustment, email the secretary in advance and it will be arranged.

How do I stand for committee?

The committee is elected at the AGM and any member can stand. Details are announced a term in advance.

Privacy notice

How we handle your information

What we collect

When you join: your name, email, college or department, status at the University, membership type, your mobile number if you choose to give it, and your volunteering interests. When you book an event: any dietary or access requirements you tell us.

Proof of nationality for free membership

Membership is free for Saudi students, so we ask for a photo of a passport, national ID or scholarship confirmation purely to check eligibility. At most two committee members see it, and it is deleted as soon as the check is done. We keep no copy and do not record the document number.

The community directory

Appearing in the community directory is entirely optional, and the committee adds each profile only with that person's explicit consent. Everything shown in a profile — name, bio, photo and any email or number — is published openly on this site, and you can ask for it to be changed or removed at any time by emailing us.

Why we hold it

To administer membership, match fee payments to your reference, run events and — if you opted in — tell you what is happening. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing. Card payments are handled by the payment provider directly; your card details never reach the Society.
